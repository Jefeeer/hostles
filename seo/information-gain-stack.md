# Information-gain stack

Use these layers in order on every future service or guide page. Each layer should add something the current search results do not already repeat.

1. Direct answer: define the service or answer the planning question in two or three sentences.
2. Lester's operating perspective: explain how he approaches pacing, transitions, tone or audience energy in his own words.
3. Original artifact: include a sample run-of-show, inquiry checklist, briefing template or annotated event-film moment.
4. Local context: explain considerations relevant to Tagaytay, Cavite or Batangas only when factual and useful.
5. Verifiable proof: link a film, public recommendation or official profile; state the source and observation date.
6. Decision support: identify who the service fits, what to prepare and which details change the quote or approach.
7. Accuracy boundary: distinguish confirmed facts from items the client must confirm, such as availability, travel and price.

## Existing stack on the site

- Homepage: identity, positioning, event films, source-linked feedback and inquiry.
- AI information page: concise facts, booking context, differentiation and accuracy policy.
